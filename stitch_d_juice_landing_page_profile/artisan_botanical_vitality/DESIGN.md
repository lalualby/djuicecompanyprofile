## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an artisan, wellness-driven spirit tailored for cold-pressed juice culture in Indonesia. It marries organic vitality with modern precision, projecting purity, craft, and understated luxury. 

The aesthetic is clean, warm-minimalist, and botanical. Interfaces rely on breathable layouts, warm sunlit neutrals, and grounded earth tones that evoke farm-fresh ingredients without feeling rustic or unpolished. Visual hierarchy is established through generous negative space, gentle transitions, and tactful micro-interactions rather than loud decorative ornament. Tone of voice balances Indonesian warmth and conversational clarity with premium professionalism ("Segar Murni Alami", "Diproses Dingin Setiap Hari").

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration directly from cold-pressed orchard harvests and botanical leaves. 

- **Primary (`#DB9558`):** A warm citrus amber that carries energy, sun-ripened fruit vibrancy, and appetite appeal. Used deliberately for primary calls-to-action, key conversion buttons, and active product focal points.
- **Secondary (`#97A87A`):** A grounded olive matcha that communicates chlorophyll freshness, health, and holistic wellness. Applied to supporting actions, secondary badges, and category labels.
- **Tertiary (`#A8BBA3`):** A soft sage green used for supportive structural elements, soft container borders, and organic pill tags.
- **Neutral (`#1F2923`):** Deep forest charcoal-green delivering maximum legibility, replacing stark black with an earthy undertone.
- **Surface Cream (`#FCF9EA`):** The primary canvas background, creating an unbleached, raw-paper warmth that avoids sterile coldness.

Use pure white (`#FFFFFF`) sparingly as elevated card surfaces floating above the cream backdrop to create crisp visual planes.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans powers the entire typographic system. Its modern geometric curves with subtle humanist touches strike the exact balance needed for an artisan wellness consumer product: approachable, premium, and clean.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in bold weights (`700` and `800`) with tight letter spacing for impactful, appetitive headers. Mobile sizes compress slightly to guarantee comfortable visual rhythm across compact screens.
- **Body:** Set at regular weight (`400`) in `#1F2923` with relaxed line heights for effortless reading of nutrition details, cold-press methodology, and artisanal sourcing.
- **Labels & Badges:** Use medium-to-bold weights with slight letter spacing expansion for Indonesian currency markers (`Rp`), product tags (`100% Organik`, `Tanpa Gula Tambahan`), and navigation labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid for desktop (max-width `1240px`), collapsing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile devices.

- **Breakpoints:** Mobile (`< 640px`), Tablet (`640px – 1024px`), Desktop (`> 1024px`).
- **Whitespace & Rhythm:** Spacing leans generous and unhurried. Outer margins breathe easily (`margin-lg: 4rem` on desktop, `margin: 1.25rem` on mobile) to let product photography stand out without visual clutter.
- **Content Flow:** Product catalog cards adhere to asymmetric 2-column or 3-column rhythms on larger displays, shifting to single- or dual-column stacked streams on smartphones.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate, sunlight-tinted ambient shadows and tonal surface stacking rather than heavy gray drops.

- **Canvas Base:** `#FCF9EA` serves as the baseline environment.
- **Resting Cards:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) layered with an ultra-soft organic shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(31, 41, 35, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(219, 149, 88, 0.03)`.
- **Hover & Active Elevation:** Surfaces gently raise with `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(31, 41, 35, 0.08)`, paired with a subtle upward translate (`-2px`).
- **Overlays & Modals:** Deep layered dialogs use a soft botanical backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) behind a high-opacity cream surface (`rgba(252, 249, 234, 0.94)`).
- **Outlines:** Low-contrast hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(168, 187, 163, 0.35)`) reinforce structure where ambient contrast needs definition.

## Shapes

The design uses soft, rounded geometries inspired by juice bottles, citrus slices, and organic forms. 

- **Primary Container Corners:** Cards and modular content panels employ `rounded-2xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`), imparting an approachable, tactile quality.
- **Inputs & Interactive Controls:** Standard form controls and secondary containers leverage `rounded-xl` (`1rem` / `16px`).
- **Pills & Chips:** Pure pill radii (`9999px`) are reserved exclusively for tags, dietary labels, counter controls, and primary action buttons, inviting touch and tap interactions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#DB9558` with `#FFFFFF` text. Fully rounded pill (`rounded-full`), padded `12px 28px`. Hover state deepens brightness by 6% with warm subtle glow.
- **Secondary:** Filled `#E5ECE0` with `#1F2923` or border-only `#97A87A` (`1.5px`) on cream background.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Text button in `#1F2923` with bold micro-arrow or botanical glyph; underlines on hover.

### Product & Content Cards
- Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface resting on `#FCF9EA`.
- Border-radius `rounded-2xl` with a hairline `1px` border of `rgba(168, 187, 163, 0.3)`.
- Internal padding of `1.25rem` (`20px`). High-contrast typography with `#DB9558` used for price callouts (`Rp 45.000`).

### Chips & Badges
- Compact pill tags with `6px 14px` padding.
- Variants:
  - *Vitality Tag:* `#F6E4D3` background with `#B86826` text (`Dingin Segar`).
  - *Botanical Benefit:* `#E5ECE0` background with `#4D5C39` text (`Detox & Imun`).
  - *Dietary Outline:* Transparent background, `1px solid #A8BBA3`, neutral dark text.

### Form Inputs
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` or `#FCF9EA` inset.
- Rounded corners (`rounded-xl`), bordered by `1.5px solid #A8BBA3`.
- Focus state shifts border to `#DB9558` with a soft halo `0 0 0 3px rgba(219, 149, 88, 0.15)`.
- Clear, warm placeholder text in `rgba(31, 41, 35, 0.45)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Rounded squares (`6px`) for checkboxes; circular for radios.
- Inactive: `#FFFFFF` surface with `1.5px solid #A8BBA3`.
- Active: Solid `#97A87A` fill with a crisp white checkmark or inner dot.

### Specialized Artisan Components
- **Ingredient Proportion Bar:** Segmented organic progress track displaying cold-press blend ratios (e.g., *Jeruk Valencia 60%*, *Wortel 30%*, *Jahe 10%*) using the palette tokens.
- **Subscription Frequency Toggle:** Two-part sliding segmented control in rounded pill containers for weekly and monthly auto-delivery schedules.
